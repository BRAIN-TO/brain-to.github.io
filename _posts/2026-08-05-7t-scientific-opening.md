Archive for the Toronto 7T MRI Opening Scientific Symposium, held on Oct 22-23, 2026 at Vaughan Estates of Sunnybrook. <!-- excerpt end -->

This inaugrual symposium marks the launch of one of only three human 7T MRI facilities in Canada and offers a first look at the research, clinical applications, and collaborative opportunities enabled by this advanced technology by bringing together leading Canadian and international experts in ultra-high-field neuroimaging.
